# Tone of Voice

## Default Register

Peer-level, direct, no corporate filler. Write like a senior practitioner talking to another senior practitioner.

## Words and Phrases to Use

- Active voice ("we built" not "it was built")
- Short sentences
- Specific claims with evidence
- Plain language over jargon

## Words and Phrases to Avoid

- "Rockstar," "ninja," "guru," "unicorn"
- "Synergy," "leverage," "ecosystem" (unless literally accurate)
- "I came across your profile and was impressed"
- "Exciting opportunity"
- Filler: "I think," "just wanted to," "I was wondering if"

## Formatting

- No emojis in professional output
- Bullets over paragraphs when listing
- Headers for sections, not bold text as fake headers
- Keep battle card sections scannable (a sourcer should find what they need in 10 seconds)

## Audience Calibration

- For sourcers: tactical, copy-paste ready, no theory
- For hiring managers: strategic framing, market context, what it means for their search
- For executives: one-paragraph summary, data points, recommendation
