# Getting Started with Snap Cup

You're 5 minutes from giving someone their flowers.

## Quick Start

1. Copy `skills/snap-cup.md` to your workspace's `.claude/skills/` folder
2. Copy `context/snap-cup-log.md` to your workspace's `context/` folder
3. Open Claude Code and run `/snap-cup`

That's it. If you have a Slack or GitHub MCP connected, the agent will scan it. If not, use Draft Mode:

> "snap cup [name] for [thing they did]"

## Your First Snap Cup

Try this right now:

**Option A (Scan Mode):** Run `/snap-cup` and let the agent look through your Slack or GitHub. It'll show you 2-4 people who did something worth acknowledging this week.

**Option B (Draft Mode):** Think of someone who did something good recently that you didn't say anything about. Tell the agent:

> "snap cup Marcus for that retry middleware he shipped last week"

The agent will draft a 1-3 sentence message you can send as a DM. Review it, adjust if needed, send.

## Tips

- **Friday afternoons** are the best time. End the week by making someone's day.
- **Level 1** is the sweet spot if you're non-technical recognizing technical work. You'll sound like you actually looked without needing to understand the code.
- **"Make it more technical"** or **"keep it simple"** adjusts the depth.
- **The cooldown log** means you'll naturally spread recognition to new people each week.
- **Pass it forward** is optional but powerful. Add it when it fits.

## What Good Looks Like

A good snap cup message is:
- 1-3 sentences
- Names the specific thing (not "your work" but "your retry logic with backoff")
- Sounds like a human, not a form letter
- Ends clean (no trailing questions or asks)

## Make It a Habit

The skill compounds. One message per week = 52 people per year who know someone noticed them. Most of them will never get that from anyone else.
